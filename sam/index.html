Questions plumbers actually ask

Straight answers, because you've heard every sales pitch in the book.

What does Sam say when he answers?
Your greeting, your company name, your rules. In regulated states (CA, CO, NY) Sam identifies as an AI system. Every greeting is written with you and recorded for your review before a single live call.
What if the customer asks if it's a robot?
Sam tells the truth, every time: he's an emergency dispatch avatar for your company, and a live dispatcher is available on request. Honesty is both the legal posture and the conversion strategy.
Does this replace my office staff?
No. Sam covers what staff can't — the 2 AM call, the third simultaneous call, the Saturday overflow. Your team keeps business hours; Sam keeps everything else.
What happens to my customer data?
It belongs to you. Exclusively. We may use anonymized data to improve the dispatcher — never your customer list, never for another plumber in your market. That's contract language, not a checkbox.
Why per-minute pricing instead of flat?
Because flat pricing is how you end up paying for a competitor's busy season. Idle in February? You pay almost nothing. Swamped in a freeze? You pay for exactly what you used — at $0.25 a minute, a $3,000 job costs you about a dollar to answer.
