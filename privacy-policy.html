Last Updated: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Answers AI LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our AI search visibility optimization services.

We collect data necessary to deliver AEO/GEO services:

  • Business Information: Company name, address, service areas, website URLs, and business descriptions.
  • Website Data: Schema markup, llms.txt content, robots.txt configuration, and site structure.
  • Analytics Data: Search visibility scores, ranking data, and performance metrics.
  • Account Data: Email, password (encrypted), billing information, and subscription details.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We integrate with the following services:

  • Google: Search Console, Analytics, Business Profile APIs

We do not sell your business data to competitors or third-party marketers.

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained for service duration

  • Audit reports: Retained for 12 months

6. Security Measures

We implement: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, OAuth 2.0 for API access, quarterly security audits, and SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure.
